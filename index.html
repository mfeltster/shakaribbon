I spent fifty years riding to die.
Now I ride to live.

This isn't a wellness brand. It's what's left after dirt bikes, VFX death sprints, ayahuasca in the Costa Rican dark, yagé nights with the Inga in Colombia, a prostate gone in 2023, and a radiation mask bolted over my face in 2024. The shaka is how my people say I see you. The ribbon is for everyone still in it. Put them together and you get the whole story. And if you count the rays in the burst, one is missing. It stays missing — for the ones we lost.

Who's Fizzle

VFX artist in a past life. Metal caster, writer, and 2X cancer survivor in this one. I make stickers and bronze medallions for the people who show up — and I'm probably the guy handing you one in a dealership parking lot.
